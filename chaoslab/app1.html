I. Logistic Map

The logistic map models population growth with resource constraints. Despite a single quadratic rule, it exhibits the full route from order to chaos.

$$x_{n+1}=r\,x_n(1-x_n)$$

Regimes:
$r<1$: extinction
$1<r<3$: stable fixed point
$r=3$: period-doubling onset
$r\approx3.449$: period-4
$r\approx3.569$: chaos onset

The Lyapunov exponent quantifies divergence rate:

$$\lambda=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}\ln\!\left|r(1-2x_n)\right|$$

$\lambda>0$ → chaotic. $\lambda<0$ → stable.

Period-doubling intervals shrink by Feigenbaum's constant:

$$\delta=\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{r_n-r_{n-1}}{r_{n+1}-r_n}\approx4.6692$$

This universal constant appears in all period-doubling routes to chaos across physics, chemistry, and engineering.

II. Lorenz System

The Lorenz system (1963) models Rayleigh-Benard convection. It introduced the concept of the butterfly effect — sensitivity to initial conditions in deterministic systems.

$$\dot x=\sigma(y-x)$$$$\dot y=x(\rho-z)-y$$$$\dot z=xy-\beta z$$

The strange attractor has fractal Hausdorff dimension $d_H\approx2.06$ — neither a surface nor a volume.

Two nearby trajectories diverge exponentially:

$$\delta(t)\approx\delta_0\,e^{\lambda_1 t},\quad\lambda_1\approx0.905$$

The system is dissipative — phase-space volumes contract globally:

$$\nabla\!\cdot\mathbf{F}=-(\sigma+1+\beta)<0$$

For $\rho<1$: origin stable. $\rho>24.74$: strange attractor. $\rho\approx99.96$: periodic windows exist within chaos.

III. Double Pendulum

Full Lagrangian equations — no small-angle approximation. State $[\theta_1,\dot\theta_1,\theta_2,\dot\theta_2]$ integrated with RK4 at $dt=0.005\,$s.

Let $\Delta=\theta_1-\theta_2$, $D=2m_1+m_2-m_2\cos(2\Delta)$.

$$\ddot\theta_1=\frac{-g(2m_1+m_2)\sin\theta_1-m_2g\sin(\theta_1-2\theta_2)-2m_2\sin\Delta[\dot\theta_2^2L_2+\dot\theta_1^2L_1\cos\Delta]}{L_1D}$$
$$\ddot\theta_2=\frac{2\sin\Delta[\dot\theta_1^2L_1(m_1+m_2)+g(m_1+m_2)\cos\theta_1+\dot\theta_2^2L_2m_2\cos\Delta]}{L_2D}$$

This is a Hamiltonian (energy-conserving) system yet strongly chaotic, proving energy conservation does not prevent chaos.

$$E=\tfrac{1}{2}(m_1+m_2)L_1^2\dot\theta_1^2+\tfrac{1}{2}m_2L_2^2\dot\theta_2^2+m_2L_1L_2\dot\theta_1\dot\theta_2\cos\Delta-(m_1+m_2)gL_1\cos\theta_1-m_2gL_2\cos\theta_2$$
IV. Driven Pendulum

The driven damped pendulum exhibits the canonical period-doubling cascade to chaos as drive amplitude $A$ increases.

$$\ddot\theta+\beta\dot\theta+\sin\theta=A\cos(\omega t)$$

State space: $(\theta,\dot\theta,t\bmod 2\pi/\omega)$. The Poincare section stroboscopically samples $(\theta,\dot\theta)$ once per drive period:

• 1 point → periodic (period-1)
• $2^n$ points → period-$2^n$
• fractal cloud → chaos

Transition (beta=0.5, omega=2/3):

$A=0.9$: period-1
$A=1.07$: period-2
$A=1.13$: period-4
$A>1.15$: chaos onset

Bifurcation ratios obey Feigenbaum scaling:

$$\frac{\Delta A_n}{\Delta A_{n+1}}\to\delta\approx4.6692$$
