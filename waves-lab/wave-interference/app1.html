The Physics of Wave Interference

Real-time mathematical simulation with independent k & ω control

1 • SUPERPOSITION PRINCIPLE

Net wave = algebraic sum of individual waves.

ψ(x, t) = ψ₁(x, t) + ψ₂(x, t)

2 • TRAVELING WAVES (k & ω independent)

Right-traveling wave

ψ₁(x, t) = A₁ sin(k₁x − ω₁t + φ₁)
vp1 = ω₁ / k₁

Left-traveling wave

ψ₂(x, t) = A₂ sin(k₂x + ω₂t + φ₂)
vp2 = −ω₂ / k₂

3 • INTERFERENCE TYPES

CONSTRUCTIVE + +

Phase difference δ = 2πm

Atotal = A₁ + A₂

DESTRUCTIVE + −

Phase difference δ = (2m+1)π

Atotal = |A₁ − A₂|

BEATS / PACKETS Δk, Δω

Envelope frequency = |Δω| / 2π

4 • STANDING WAVES

ψ(x, t) = 2A cos(kx) sin(ωt)

5 • GROUP VELOCITY VISUALIZATION + DISPERSION RELATION (ENHANCED)

The envelope of the superposition wave travels at the group velocity vg ≈ (ω₂ − ω₁) / (k₂ − k₁).
Individual wave crests travel at their phase velocities vp = ω/k.

Envelope = √(A₁² + A₂² + 2A₁A₂ cos(Δkx − Δωt + Δφ))
