Igo

IgoBreaker: one nucleus–nucleus potential fitted simultaneously to bound, quasi-bound and elastic-scattering data. Woods–Saxon, WS², Michel or M3Y double-folding shapes, EXFOR/ENSDF data retrieval, sensitivity and Fisher analysis, family scans and global fits to break the Igo ambiguity.

26

CC

Coupled-channels solver on a Lagrange-mesh R-matrix: inelastic excitation with collective structure models, DWBA and CRC transfer, two-step and simultaneous two-nucleon transfer, CDCC breakup with continuum bins, a FRESCO input map and least-squares fitting to data.

27

Inversion 1

Inverse Scattering Workbench: determining the optical potential from elastic-scattering data. Direct problem (radial equation, S-matrix, observables), EXFOR angular-distribution retrieval, automatic potential-model ranking and χ² fits, χ² landscape and potential families, single-energy phase-shift analysis with iterative-perturbative, Lipperheide–Fiedeldey and Newton–Sabatier inversion, multi-energy (mixed-case) inversion, and fusion data to pin down the barrier.

Inversion 2
