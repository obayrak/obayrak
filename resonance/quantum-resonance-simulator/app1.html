01The Two-Level System

A two-level quantum system (qubit) has only two eigenstates: the ground state |g⟩ and the excited state |e⟩, separated by energy ℏω₀. The general pure state is:

|ψ⟩ = α|g⟩ + β|e⟩   |α|²+|β|²=1

The density matrix ρ = |ψ⟩⟨ψ| fully describes mixed states too. Its diagonal elements ρ_gg, ρ_ee give the populations; off-diagonals ρ_ge, ρ_eg are the coherences.

ρ = [[ρ_gg, ρ_ge], [ρ_eg, ρ_ee]] (Tr ρ = 1)

Examples: qubit in quantum computers, NMR spin-½, two-photon atom, superconducting transmon, nitrogen-vacancy center in diamond.

02Driving & Rabi Oscillations

Apply an oscillating field at frequency ω. In the rotating wave approximation (RWA), the Hamiltonian in the rotating frame is:

H_RWA = −(ℏ/2)[Δ·σz + Ω·σx]

where Δ = ω − ω₀ is the detuning and Ω is the Rabi coupling (proportional to field amplitude × dipole moment).

At resonance Δ = 0, the system oscillates completely between |g⟩ and |e⟩ at the Rabi frequency Ω. The excited-state population:

P_e(t) = (Ω/Ω_R)² · sin²(Ω_R·t/2)
Ω_R = √(Ω² + Δ²)   (generalized Rabi frequency)
03Bloch Sphere & Equations

Any two-level state maps to a point on the Bloch sphere via the real Bloch vector (u, v, w):

u = 2·Re(ρ_eg)   v = 2·Im(ρ_eg)   w = ρ_ee − ρ_gg

Pure states lie on the surface (|r|=1); mixed states are inside. The Bloch equations govern the time evolution in the rotating frame:

du/dt = Δv   − u/T₂
dv/dt = −Δu + Ωw − v/T₂
dw/dt = −Ωv   − (w+1)/T₁

The Rabi drive rotates the Bloch vector; relaxation shrinks it toward the ground state.

04Relaxation: T₁ and T₂

T₁ — longitudinal relaxation (energy relaxation): the rate at which the population decays to equilibrium. The excited state decays as:

P_e(t) = P_e(0) · e^(−t/T₁)

T₂ — transverse relaxation (dephasing): coherences (u, v) decay due to phase noise. Always T₂ ≤ 2T₁. The coherence length:

|ρ_eg|(t) = |ρ_eg(0)| · e^(−t/T₂)

The resonance linewidth (FWHM) is determined by T₂: Γ = 1/T₂. A pure state has T₂ = 2T₁; in practice dephasing makes T₂ < 2T₁. The steady-state Lorentzian lineshape:

P_e^ss(Δ) = (Ω²·T₁T₂/2) / (1 + Δ²T₂² + Ω²T₁T₂)

The Full Bloch Equations (rotating frame, RWA)

In the interaction picture (rotating frame at drive frequency ω), after the rotating wave approximation eliminates fast oscillations at ω+ω₀, the density matrix equations reduce to the optical Bloch equations:

du/dt = +Δ·v − u/T₂
dv/dt = −Δ·u + Ω·w − v/T₂
dw/dt = −Ω·v − (w+1)/T₁

This system is integrated numerically using 4th-order Runge-Kutta (RK4) with Δt = 0.005 Rabi units. The Bloch vector (u, v, w) maps to a point on the unit sphere; w = ρ_ee − ρ_gg is the population inversion.

Steady-State Solution

Setting all time derivatives to zero gives the optical Bloch steady state:

w_ss = −(1 + Δ²T₂²) / (1 + Δ²T₂² + Ω²T₁T₂)

The excited-state population P_e^ss = (1+w_ss)/2 forms a Lorentzian lineshape centred at Δ=0 with width Γ = 1/T₂ (in Rabi units). This is exactly the lineshape shown in the top-right chart.

Rabi Oscillation (Δ=0, no relaxation)

The textbook quantum resonance scenario. The state vector precesses around the x-axis of the Bloch sphere. At Δ=0, the Rabi frequency Ω_R = Ω and the population undergoes complete inversion: P_e oscillates between 0 and 1. A π-pulse (time T = π/Ω) flips the qubit completely from |g⟩ to |e⟩.

Off-Resonance (Δ ≠ 0)

When the drive detuning is non-zero, the generalized Rabi frequency increases: Ω_R = √(Ω²+Δ²). The oscillation is faster but the population never fully inverts — maximum P_e = (Ω/Ω_R)² < 1. On the Bloch sphere, the trajectory traces a smaller circle offset from the equator.

T₁ Decay (energy relaxation)

With Ω=0 and starting in |e⟩, the population decays exponentially: P_e(t) = e^(−t/T₁). On the Bloch sphere, the state vector spirals from the north pole (|e⟩) straight down to the south pole (|g⟩).

T₂ Dephasing (coherence loss)

Starting in a superposition |+x⟩ with no drive, the coherences u, v decay as e^(−t/T₂) while w remains fixed. The Bloch vector shrinks radially inward — the qubit loses its quantum phase information but keeps its population.

Strong Drive (power broadening)

When Ω >> 1/T₂, the resonance line broadens beyond its natural linewidth — power broadening. The Lorentzian half-width becomes √(1+Ω²T₁T₂)/T₂. The Bloch sphere motion becomes very fast.

Bloch Sphere

  • North pole (0,0,+1) = |e⟩ fully excited state. South pole (0,0,−1) = |g⟩ ground state.
  • Equator = maximum coherence, equal superposition of |g⟩ and |e⟩. Different phases correspond to different equatorial points.
  • Interior = mixed states (decoherence has occurred). The Bloch vector shrinks from the surface toward the centre.
  • Gold arrow = current state vector (u, v, w). The trail shows its history.
