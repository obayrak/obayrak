STANDING WAVE — SUPERPOSITIONFINITE DIFFERENCE · WAVE EQ
NODE / ANTINODE DISPLACEMENT MAPAMPLITUDE FIELD

Standing waves arise from the superposition of two identical waves travelling in opposite directions. At nodes the displacement is always zero; at antinodes the amplitude is maximum (2A). The wave equation ∂²y/∂t² = v²·∂²y/∂x² is solved numerically. Resonance occurs when the pipe/string length is an integer multiple of half-wavelengths.

Open pipe (both ends): antinodes at both ends → fₙ = n·v/(2L) — all harmonics present.
Closed pipe (one end): node at closed end, antinode at open end → fₙ = (2n−1)·v/(4L) — only ODD harmonics.

REAL WORLD: OPERA SINGER + GLASS & BRIDGES
🎭Glass shattering: A wine glass has a natural frequency ~550 Hz with Q ≈ 1000. When driven at resonance, amplitude grows each cycle. At resonance A = Q·F₀/(mω₀²) — the Q-factor multiplies the static deflection. Energy accumulates until stress exceeds the elastic limit, fracturing the glass.
🌉Tacoma Narrows (1940): Wind caused aeroelastic flutter at the bridge's torsional natural frequency (0.2 Hz). The structure had insufficient damping (Q very high), allowing resonance to build catastrophic oscillations. Modern bridges include dampers to reduce Q.

COUPLED OSCILLATORS — ENERGY TRANSFERRK4 · 4-DOF SYSTEM
ENERGY DISTRIBUTION OVER TIMEKINETIC + POTENTIAL

Coupled oscillators exchange energy through a coupling spring k_c. The system has two normal modes: in-phase (symmetric) ω₋ = √(k/m) and out-of-phase (antisymmetric) ω₊ = √((k+2k_c)/m). Starting with one mass displaced, energy transfers completely to the second and returns — the period of exchange is T_beat = π/(ω₊−ω₋). The blue/magenta traces show each oscillator's displacement; the energy panel reveals the periodic energy swap.

REAL-WORLD: RESONANCE COUPLING
⚛NMR / MRI: Nuclear spins are coupled quantum oscillators. RF pulses at the Larmor frequency transfer energy to nuclei, then the relaxation signal is detected — enabling non-invasive medical imaging through resonant energy exchange.
🎸Sympathetic strings: Sitar, piano, and viola d'amore strings vibrate sympathetically when another string's pitch matches their natural frequency — acoustic resonance coupling at work.
🔌Wireless power / inductive charging: Resonant LC circuits tuned to the same frequency achieve near-100% power transfer efficiency at distance — Kurs et al. (2007) demonstrated 60% efficiency at 2 m separation.

ACTIVE NOISE CANCELLATION — DESTRUCTIVE INTERFERENCEANC SIMULATION
CANCELLATION EFFICIENCY METER

Active Noise Cancellation (ANC) generates a signal precisely 180° out of phase with the noise: y_cancel = −y_noise, so y_noise + y_cancel = 0. Perfect cancellation requires matched amplitude AND exact phase. A phase error ε gives residual = 2A·sin(ε/2). An amplitude error δ gives residual = |1−δ|A. ANC is most effective for low-frequency, steady-state noise below ~1 kHz — higher frequencies have shorter wavelengths that outpace the controller's reaction time. Adjust the phase and amplitude offsets to observe imperfect cancellation.

REAL WORLD: ANC IN PRACTICE
🎧ANC headphones: A feedforward microphone samples ambient noise; a DSP inverts it in <1 ms; a speaker plays the anti-noise inside the ear cup. Effective for constant low-frequency noise (aircraft cabin hum ~80-400 Hz). Impulsive noise cannot be cancelled.
✈Aircraft cabin / automotive: Honda and Bose use ANC inside car cabins with multiple microphones and speakers, cancelling road and engine noise. The controller must adapt in real-time as road conditions change.
🏭Industrial HVAC ducts: For large duct diameters, passive absorption fails at low frequencies (λ ≫ duct diameter). ANC speakers flush-mounted in ducts cancel fan noise at the plane-wave frequencies below the first cutoff frequency.
PHASE PORTRAIT — POSITION vs VELOCITYPHASE SPACE · HAMILTONIAN DYNAMICS
ENERGY vs TIME — HAMILTONIAN H(t)KE + PE PARTITION

Phase space maps every system state (x, ẋ) as a single point. Undamped free oscillation → closed ellipse (constant energy H). Damped free oscillation → inward spiral to origin. Driven at resonance → outward spiral then limit cycle. The Hamiltonian H = ½mẋ² + ½kx² = KE + PE; for undamped oscillators H is conserved, and each ellipse in phase space is a constant-energy contour. In the energy plot, KE and PE oscillate 90° out of phase while their sum H stays constant.
