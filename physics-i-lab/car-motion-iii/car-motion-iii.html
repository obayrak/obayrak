1 · Equation of Motion

This simulator models 1-D car motion on a road with incline angle θ. The car is influenced by engine traction, aerodynamic drag, rolling resistance, and gravity.

F_drivePropulsive / braking force
F_airAerodynamic drag: ½ρCdAv²
F_rollRolling resistance: μrmg cosθ
F_gravSlope gravity: mg sinθ
Equation of motion
m a = F_drive − ½ρC_d A v|v| − μ_r m g cos(θ) sgn(v) − m g sin(θ)
Acceleration form
dv/dt = [F_drive − F_air − F_roll − F_grav] / m
Integrated numerically using 4th-order Runge–Kutta.
