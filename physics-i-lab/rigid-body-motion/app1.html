Every rigid body on the track has two independent sets of degrees of freedom: translational motion of its center of mass along the track (position x/arc-length s, velocity v, acceleration a), and rotational motion about its own center (angle θ, angular velocity ω, angular acceleration α). The two columns below are direct analogues of each other — every translational law on the left has a rotational mirror on the right, with mass m ↔ moment of inertia I and force F ↔ torque τ swapping roles. When rolling without slipping, the two sides are coupled by the constraint v = ωR.
