∇f Gradient
\[\nabla f=\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}\,\hat{\imath}+\frac{\partial f}{\partial y}\,\hat{\jmath}+\frac{\partial f}{\partial z}\,\hat{k}\] \[\text{Time-dependent:}\quad\frac{\partial(\nabla f)}{\partial t}=\nabla\!\left(\frac{\partial f}{\partial t}\right)\]
EM \(\mathbf E=-\nabla\varphi\) electric field from potential. MECH \(\mathbf F=-\nabla U\) conservative force. FLUID \(\nabla p\) pressure gradient drives flow.
\[\begin{aligned} \text{Coulomb:}\;&\mathbf E=-\nabla\!\left(\frac{q}{4\pi\varepsilon_0 r}\right)\;\Rightarrow\;\mathbf E=\frac{q\,\hat r}{4\pi\varepsilon_0 r^2}\\ \text{Gravity:}\;&\mathbf g=-\nabla\Phi,\quad\Phi=-\frac{GM}{r}\\ \text{Bernoulli:}\;&\tfrac12\rho v^2+p+\rho g z=\text{const} \end{aligned}\]
∇·F Divergence
\[\nabla\!\cdot\!\mathbf F=\frac{\partial F_x}{\partial x}+\frac{\partial F_y}{\partial y}+\frac{\partial F_z}{\partial z}\] \[\text{Gauss:}\quad\oiint_{S}\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf A=\iiint_V(\nabla\!\cdot\!\mathbf F)\,dV\]
MAXWELL \(\nabla\!\cdot\!\mathbf E=\rho/\varepsilon_0\) (Gauss's law). \(\nabla\!\cdot\!\mathbf B=0\) (no monopoles). FLUID \(\partial\rho/\partial t+\nabla\!\cdot\!(\rho\mathbf v)=0\) (continuity).
\[\begin{aligned} \nabla\!\cdot\!\mathbf F\gt0:&\quad\text{source (field lines emerge)}\\ \nabla\!\cdot\!\mathbf F\lt0:&\quad\text{sink (field lines converge)}\\ \nabla\!\cdot\!\mathbf F=0:&\quad\text{solenoidal / incompressible} \end{aligned}\]
∇×F Curl / Rotation
\[\begin{aligned} \nabla\times\mathbf F=&\left(\frac{\partial F_z}{\partial y}-\frac{\partial F_y}{\partial z}\right)\hat{\imath}\\ &+\left(\frac{\partial F_x}{\partial z}-\frac{\partial F_z}{\partial x}\right)\hat{\jmath}\\ &+\left(\frac{\partial F_y}{\partial x}-\frac{\partial F_x}{\partial y}\right)\hat{k} \end{aligned}\] \[\text{Stokes:}\quad\oint_C\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf l=\iint_S(\nabla\times\mathbf F)\cdot d\mathbf A\]
MAXWELL \(\nabla\times\mathbf E=-\partial\mathbf B/\partial t\) (Faraday). \(\nabla\times\mathbf B=\mu_0\mathbf J+\mu_0\varepsilon_0\,\partial\mathbf E/\partial t\) (Ampère–Maxwell). FLUID \(\vec\omega=\nabla\times\mathbf v\) (vorticity). PLASMA \(\nabla\times\mathbf B=\mu_0\mathbf J\) current density.
∂/∂t Time Dependence
\[\begin{aligned} \text{Material derivative:}&\quad\frac{Df}{Dt}=\frac{\partial f}{\partial t}+\mathbf v\cdot\nabla f\\ \text{Wave operator:}&\quad\Box=\frac{\partial^2}{\partial t^2}-c^2\nabla^2\\ \text{Maxwell wave:}&\quad\Box\mathbf E=0\ \text{(free space)} \end{aligned}\]
TIME All fields in this lab use variable t (seconds). The time slider and play button animate the field evolution. Use \(\partial f/\partial t\) in the Analysis tab to see the temporal rate of change.

Key Identities
\[\begin{aligned} \nabla\cdot(\nabla\times\mathbf F)&=0&&\text{(curl is solenoidal)}\\ \nabla\times(\nabla f)&=0&&\text{(gradient is irrotational)}\\ \nabla\times(\nabla\times\mathbf F)&=\nabla(\nabla\cdot\mathbf F)-\nabla^2\mathbf F&&\text{(vector Laplacian)}\\ \nabla(fg)&=f\nabla g+g\nabla f\\ \nabla\cdot(f\mathbf F)&=f\,\nabla\cdot\mathbf F+\mathbf F\cdot\nabla f\\ \frac{\partial\rho}{\partial t}+\nabla\cdot(\rho\mathbf v)&=0&&\text{(continuity)} \end{aligned}\]
Numerical Method
Central differences with step \(h=10^{-4}\), error \(O(h^2)\):
\[\begin{aligned} \frac{\partial f}{\partial x}&\approx\frac{f(x+h,y,z)-f(x-h,y,z)}{2h}\\ \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x^2}&\approx\frac{f(x+h)-2f(x)+f(x-h)}{h^2}\\ \frac{\partial f}{\partial t}&\approx\frac{f(t+h_t)-f(t-h_t)}{2h_t},\quad h_t=0.01 \end{aligned}\]
Arrow update is throttled to \(\le 20\) fps during play.

▸ EXPRESSION SYNTAX
Variables: x, y, z — spatial coords. t — time. a, b, c, k — adjustable parameters. Pre-computed: r = \(\sqrt{x^2+y^2+z^2}\), rxy = \(\sqrt{x^2+y^2}\), pi, e.
