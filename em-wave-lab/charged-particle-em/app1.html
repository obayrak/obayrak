PHYSICS OF CHARGED PARTICLES IN EM FIELDS
1 — The Lorentz Force Law

The complete force acting on a particle with charge q and velocity 𝐯 in combined electric and magnetic fields is the Lorentz force:

Lorentz Force
𝐅 = q ( 𝐄 + 𝐯 × 𝐁 )

The electric term q𝐄 acts along the field and does work on the particle. The magnetic term q(𝐯 × 𝐁) is always perpendicular to the velocity, so it deflects but never accelerates — the magnetic force does zero work, meaning it cannot change kinetic energy.

2 — Equations of Motion

Applying Newton's second law 𝐅 = m𝐚 gives a coupled first-order ODE system in the state vector 𝐲 = (x, y, z, vₓ, vᵧ, v_z):

State-Space ODEs
d𝐫/dt = 𝐯
d𝐯/dt = (q/m)( 𝐄 + 𝐯 × 𝐁 )
Component Form (Cartesian)
dvₓ/dt = (q/m)[ Eₓ + vᵧ B_z − v_z Bᵧ ]
dvᵧ/dt = (q/m)[ Eᵧ + v_z Bₓ − vₓ B_z ]
dv_z/dt = (q/m)[ E_z + vₓ Bᵧ − vᵧ Bₓ ]

The cross-coupling through the magnetic field makes this system non-trivial — the component equations are mutually dependent through the velocity components.

3 — Numerical Integration: 4th-Order Runge-Kutta

This simulator uses RK4, which balances accuracy and efficiency. For state 𝐲ₙ at time tₙ:

RK4 Step
k₁ = f(𝐲ₙ)
k₂ = f(𝐲ₙ + ½Δt · k₁)
k₃ = f(𝐲ₙ + ½Δt · k₂)
k₄ = f(𝐲ₙ + Δt · k₃)
𝐲ₙ₊₁ = 𝐲ₙ + (Δt/6)(k₁ + 2k₂ + 2k₃ + k₄)

RK4 has global error O(Δt⁴). The critical stability condition for cyclotron orbits is ωc · Δt ≪ 1, where ωc = |q|B/m. Reducing Δt improves both accuracy and energy conservation.

4 — Pure Magnetic Field: Circular and Helical Motion

With 𝐄 = 0, the magnetic force acts as centripetal force. For velocity 𝐯⊥ perpendicular to 𝐁, the particle executes uniform circular motion in the plane perpendicular to 𝐁:

Cyclotron (Larmor) Frequency
ωc = |q||𝐁| / m [rad/s in SI]
Larmor (Cyclotron) Radius
rL = m v⊥ / (|q||𝐁|) = v⊥ / ωc

If the velocity has a component v∥ along 𝐁, the particle moves in a helix: it rotates in the transverse plane (circle of radius rL) while drifting along 𝐁 unchanged. The helix pitch is p = 2π v∥ / ωc. Energy is conserved: |𝐯|² = v⊥² + v∥² = const.

The sign of q determines the helicity (left-hand vs right-hand spiral). For the same |q|/m, a proton and an electron orbit in opposite senses.

5 — Pure Electric Field: Parabolic Motion

With 𝐁 = 0 and uniform 𝐄, the particle experiences constant acceleration 𝐚 = q𝐄/m. This is exactly analogous to projectile motion in gravity:

Trajectory (uniform E)
𝐫(t) = 𝐫₀ + 𝐯₀ t + ½ (q𝐄/m) t²

The work-energy theorem applies: ΔKE = q · ΔV, where ΔV is the electrostatic potential difference. The trajectory is parabolic in the plane containing 𝐄 and 𝐯₀.

6 — Crossed Fields: E × B Drift

When 𝐄 ⊥ 𝐁, all particles regardless of mass, charge sign, or speed drift with the same velocity perpendicular to both fields:

E × B Drift Velocity
𝐯_d = (𝐄 × 𝐁) / |𝐁|²

This charge-independent drift is fundamental in plasma physics. Superimposed on the cyclotron motion, it produces cycloidal trajectories. A particle starting at rest traces a cycloid — forward arcs linked by cusps. The drift speed is purely |E|/|B|, independent of q/m. This is the basis of the velocity selector and the magnetron device.

7 — Gradient and Curvature Drifts

In non-uniform magnetic fields (not modeled here for uniform fields), two additional drifts arise:

DriftOriginFormula
∇B driftGradient of |B| across orbit radius𝐯_∇B = (mv⊥²/2qB³) 𝐁 × ∇B
Curvature driftCentrifugal force from following curved field lines𝐯_c = (mv∥²/qB²R²) 𝐑 × 𝐁

These drifts are charge-dependent and drive current sheets in planetary magnetospheres and tokamak plasmas. They are responsible for the azimuthal drift of radiation belt particles in Earth's magnetic field.

8 — Energy Conservation as a Numerical Benchmark
Field ConfigConserved QuantityRK4 Signature
Pure BKE = ½m|𝐯|² (B does no work)|v| stays constant to O(Δt⁴)
Pure EKE + PE = ½mv² + qVTotal energy conserved
EB combinedCanonical momentum p − qAViolated by non-symplectic integrators

For pure magnetic fields, energy conservation is a direct test of integration quality. If |v| drifts over many cyclotron periods, reduce Δt. Symplectic integrators (Boris algorithm, Störmer-Verlet) preserve the Hamiltonian structure exactly and are preferred in long-time plasma simulations over RK4.
