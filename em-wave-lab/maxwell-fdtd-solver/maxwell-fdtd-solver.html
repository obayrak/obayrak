FDTD MAXWELL SOLVER

This simulator solves Maxwell's curl equations directly using the Finite-Difference Time-Domain (FDTD) Yee algorithm. The fields are updated at every time step from the actual PDE — no analytical solutions or shortcuts.

Maxwell's Curl Equations (source-free)

\[\begin{aligned}\nabla\times\mathbf E&=-\mu_0\frac{\partial\mathbf H}{\partial t}\\ \nabla\times\mathbf H&=\varepsilon_0\frac{\partial\mathbf E}{\partial t}\end{aligned}\]

1-D Reduction (propagation along ẑ)

For a TEM wave with E in the x-y plane and no x,y variation:

\[\begin{aligned}\frac{\partial H_y}{\partial t}&=-\frac{1}{\mu_0}\frac{\partial E_x}{\partial z}, &\quad \frac{\partial E_x}{\partial t}&=-\frac{1}{\varepsilon_0}\frac{\partial H_y}{\partial z}\\[4pt] \frac{\partial H_x}{\partial t}&=+\frac{1}{\mu_0}\frac{\partial E_y}{\partial z}, &\quad \frac{\partial E_y}{\partial t}&=+\frac{1}{\varepsilon_0}\frac{\partial H_x}{\partial z}\end{aligned}\]

Both polarization components propagate independently — superposition holds exactly.

Yee Discretization (Normalized: \(c=\Delta z/\Delta t=1\))

// Half-step H update (n → n+½) Hy[i] = Hy[i] − (Ex[i+1] − Ex[i]) Hx[i] = Hx[i] + (Ey[i+1] − Ey[i])   // Full-step E update (n → n+1) Ex[i] = Ex[i] − (Hy[i] − Hy[i-1]) Ey[i] = Ey[i] + (Hx[i] − Hx[i-1])
Courant condition \(c\,\Delta t/\Delta z\le 1\) ensures stability. Here \(c\,\Delta t/\Delta z=1\) exactly \(\Rightarrow\) numerically exact wave speed.

Perfectly Matched Layer (PML)

The boundaries use exponentially graded conductivity \(\sigma(d)=\sigma_{\max}(d/D)^3\). Fields in the PML satisfy \(E_{\mathrm{pml}}\to E\,e^{-\sigma t}\), suppressing artificial reflections. 25-cell PML is used on both ends.

POLARIZATION THEORY

General Elliptical Polarization

\[\mathbf E(z,t)=A_x\cos(kz-\omega t+\varphi_x)\,\hat{\mathbf x}+A_y\cos(kz-\omega t+\varphi_y)\,\hat{\mathbf y}\]

The tip of \(\mathbf E\) at any fixed \(z\) traces an ellipse in the transverse plane. Special cases:

Linear: \(A_x\) or \(A_y=0\), or \(\Delta\varphi=0\) or \(\pi\)
Circular: \(A_x=A_y,\ \Delta\varphi=\pm\pi/2\)
Elliptical: all other cases

Jones Calculus

\[\mathbf J=\begin{pmatrix}A_x e^{j\varphi_x}\\ A_y e^{j\varphi_y}\end{pmatrix}\]

Jones matrices describe polarization-transforming optical elements. Wave plates, polarizers, and rotators are all 2×2 complex matrices.

Stokes Parameters

\[\begin{aligned}S_0&=A_x^2+A_y^2 &&\text{(total intensity)}\\ S_1&=A_x^2-A_y^2 &&\text{(linear H/V preference)}\\ S_2&=2A_xA_y\cos\delta &&\text{(linear }\pm45^\circ\text{ preference)}\\ S_3&=2A_xA_y\sin\delta &&\text{(circular preference)}\end{aligned}\]

where \(\delta=\varphi_y-\varphi_x\). The Poincaré sphere has \(S_1,S_2,S_3\) as Cartesian axes. Any fully polarized state maps to its surface.

North pole: RHCP (\(\delta=+\pi/2,\ S_3=+S_0\))
South pole: LHCP (\(\delta=-\pi/2,\ S_3=-S_0\))
Equator: all linear states

Orientation & Ellipticity

\[\begin{aligned}\tan 2\psi&=S_2/S_1 &&\Rightarrow\ \psi\in[0^\circ,180^\circ]\\ \sin 2\chi&=S_3/S_0 &&\Rightarrow\ \chi\in[-45^\circ,+45^\circ]\end{aligned}\]

Poynting Vector

\[\mathbf S=\mathbf E\times\mathbf H\quad[\mathrm{W/m^2}]\]\[\langle I\rangle=\tfrac12\,\varepsilon_0 c\,(A_x^2+A_y^2)\]

Both polarization components contribute to the total power. The FDTD correctly propagates total field energy.

Wave Equation

\[\nabla^2\mathbf E=\frac{1}{c^2}\frac{\partial^2\mathbf E}{\partial t^2}\ \Rightarrow\ \mathbf E=\mathbf E_0\,e^{j(kz-\omega t)}\]
\[c=\frac{1}{\sqrt{\mu_0\varepsilon_0}}=299\,792\,458\ \mathrm{m/s}\]

NUMERICAL ACCURACY

Phase Velocity Error

The FDTD numerical phase velocity for a 1D Yee grid with Courant number \(S=c\,\Delta t/\Delta z\):

\[v_{\mathrm{ph}}=\frac{\omega}{k}=\frac{2}{k\,\Delta t}\sin^{-1}\!\Big(S\,\sin\frac{k\Delta z}{2}\Big)\]

At \(S=1\) exactly, \(v_{\mathrm{ph}}=c\) (no numerical dispersion in 1D). This is why we choose \(c\,\Delta t/\Delta z=1\).

Dispersion Relation

\[\frac{\sin^2(\omega\Delta t/2)}{(c\,\Delta t)^2}=\frac{\sin^2(k\Delta z/2)}{(\Delta z)^2}\]

For \(S=1\) this reduces to \(\omega\Delta t/2=k\Delta z/2\), giving the exact continuous dispersion \(\omega=ck\). All wavelengths travel at exactly \(c\).

Source Injection (Soft Source)

The source is injected additively at cell \(i_{\mathrm{src}}=60\):

// Applied each step n: Ex[ISRC] += Ax·sin(ω·n)·ramp(n) Ey[ISRC] += Ay·sin(ω·n+δ)·ramp(n) ramp(n) = tanh(n·fc/1.5) // smooth startup

Soft source generates waves in both ±z. The −z wave is absorbed by the left PML. The +z wave propagates to fill the domain.

Energy Conservation

\[\frac{d}{dt}\int\tfrac12\left(\varepsilon_0|\mathbf E|^2+\mu_0|\mathbf H|^2\right)dV=-\oint\mathbf S\cdot d\mathbf A\]

Total EM energy change equals Poynting flux through boundaries. PML gradually absorbs energy at the edges, preventing artificial reflections.

Grid resolution: \(\lambda/\Delta z\ge 1/f_c=40\) cells/wavelength (well above the minimum 10 cells/\(\lambda\) required for accuracy).

FIBER OPTICS

Optical fibers guide polarized EM waves via total internal reflection. Understanding polarization from Maxwell's equations is fundamental to fiber design.

Polarization Mode Dispersion (PMD)

Real fibers have slight birefringence (different phase velocities for x and y polarizations), causing the two components to arrive at different times. This limits bandwidth in long-haul links.

\[\langle\Delta\tau\rangle=D_{\mathrm{PMD}}\sqrt{L}\]Typical SMF: \(D_{\mathrm{PMD}}\approx 0.1\ \mathrm{ps}/\sqrt{\mathrm{km}}\)

Polarization-Maintaining Fiber (PMF)

Deliberately strong birefringence (Panda, Bow-Tie fiber) keeps the polarization stable over long distances. Essential for fiber gyroscopes and coherent communications.

Coherent Detection & Polarization Diversity

Modern 100G+ DWDM systems use dual-polarization QPSK, recovering both Ex and Ey quadratures. Stokes parameters are measured in real-time by the coherent receiver DSP.

Capacity: Dual-pol 16-QAM at 600 Gbps per wavelength (2024 commercial record)

Fiber Lasers

The output polarization of a fiber laser depends on the cavity birefringence and gain medium. FDTD simulation of the fiber cross-section determines the guided modes.

ANTENNA POLARIZATION

Antenna polarization directly corresponds to the E-field orientation the antenna radiates. Polarization mismatch causes signal loss set by \(|\hat{\mathbf e}_{rx}\cdot\hat{\mathbf e}_{tx}|^2\).

Polarization Loss Factor (PLF)

\[\mathrm{PLF}=|\hat{\mathbf e}_{tx}\cdot\hat{\mathbf e}_{rx}|^2\]Vertical \(\perp\) horizontal: \(\mathrm{PLF}=0\ \Rightarrow\ -\infty\) dB loss!

Circularly Polarized Antennas

GPS, satellite TV, and many radar systems use circular polarization. RHCP antenna receives RHCP with \(\mathrm{PLF}=1\) but LHCP with \(\mathrm{PLF}=0\). Circular polarization is robust to antenna rotation.

RHCP patch: two feeds with \(90^\circ\) hybrid coupler
Axial ratio \(\le 3\) dB for “good” circular polarization

Polarimetric Radar

Transmit H, receive both H and V (or transmit both simultaneously). The \(2\times2\) scattering matrix \(\begin{bmatrix}S_{hh}&S_{hv}\\ S_{vh}&S_{vv}\end{bmatrix}\) fully characterizes the target's polarimetric response.

Dual-pol weather radar identifies rain (spherical drops: ZDR≈0 dB) vs hail (tumbling: ZDR≈0 dB but different LDR) vs snow (aligned crystals: high ZDR)

5G Massive MIMO Polarization

Cross-polarized antenna elements (±45°) double the number of spatial streams. The FDTD-computed element patterns determine the full array radiation characteristics.

FURTHER APPLICATIONS

Quantum Optics & Entanglement

Photon polarization is a qubit. Bell states are maximally entangled polarization pairs. The Stokes parameters have quantum analogues in the density matrix formalism:

\[\rho=\tfrac12\left(I+\vec S\cdot\vec\sigma\right)\]where \(\vec\sigma\) are the Pauli matrices

Ellipsometry

Measure how reflection changes polarization (ψ, Δ) to characterize thin film thickness and refractive index to sub-nm precision. Used for semiconductor process control.

\[\tan\psi\,e^{j\Delta}=\frac{r_p}{r_s}\quad\text{(Fresnel ratios)}\]

Optical Coherence Tomography (OCT)

Polarization-sensitive OCT adds another contrast channel beyond intensity: local birefringence reveals tissue structure (collagen, nerve fibers, retinal layers) invisible in standard OCT.

LCD & OLED Displays

LCDs work entirely on polarization control: backlight → linear polarizer → liquid crystal (variable waveplate) → crossed analyzer. Every pixel is a FDTD-designable polarization rotator.

FDTD is the primary design tool for photonic crystals, integrated photonic circuits, metasurfaces, and near-field optics — everywhere Maxwell matters at the wavelength scale.

MRI

The RF coils excite a circularly polarized \(B_1\) field at the Larmor frequency. Only one circular polarization (co-rotating with precessing spins) is absorbed — the other is rejected. This halves the required RF power vs linear polarization.
